HOW TO USE THESE TABLES

Tap a column name to sort by it. Use the boxes to search for a place, or filter by age (kid / grown-up) and area. Tick the box when you find one — your checks save on your tablet, and the counter at the top of each table shows how many you have left.

KID TIP! Do these as you play, not all at the end! Each chapter of the main guide has a yellow "While you're here" box that tells you which Heart Pieces and Skulltulas to grab right now, while you are already in that place.

❤️ ALL 36 HEART PIECES

4 pieces = 1 new heart. All 36 = 9 extra hearts. With the 8 boss Heart Containers, that is 20 hearts — the max!

🕷️ ALL 100 GOLD SKULLTULAS

Kill the gold spider, grab its token, and take tokens to the House of Skulltula in Kakariko Village. Most outside spiders only come out at night (play the Sun's Song). Dungeon spiders are out any time. Some hide in trees — hit or run into the tree! Some hide under soft dirt — drop a bug from a bottle on the patch.

Prizes: 10 tokens = Adult's Wallet (500 rupees) · 20 = Echo Gem (glows near spiders you missed!) · 30 = Giant's Wallet (1,000 rupees) · 40 = 20 Bombchus · 50 = a Heart Piece · 100 = Gold Rupee (200 rupees, every visit!)
