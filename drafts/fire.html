9. FIRE TEMPLE

Hot, hot, hot! Bring a Goron Tunic so the fire does not hurt you.

Goal: Wake Darunia, the Sage of Fire. Free all the Gorons locked in cells!

There are 9 Gorons locked in cells in this temple. Each freed Goron leaves you a Small Key. Free them all!

BEFORE YOU GO IN

  1. Go to Goron City on Death Mountain. Buy the Goron Tunic in the shop and put it on! It costs a lot of rupees, so save up first. Without it, the hot rooms hurt you.
  2. Sheik meets you and teaches you the Bolero of Fire. This song warps you to the Fire Temple.
  3. Enter the Fire Temple in the volcano crater. It is a maze of hot rooms and lava!

THE FIRST GORON

  1. The entry hall has four doors. One is locked. One is blocked by a big statue. One leads to a Goron cell you cannot open yet. Go up the stairs to the west (left).
  2. You find a big room with a lava pit. Your friend Darunia is here! He says he will fight the dragon Volvagia alone. He wants you to free his 9 Goron friends first. He leaves through the far door. You cannot jump that far. Go back down.
  3. From the door, go south. Hop across the platforms over the lava to the far ledge. A Goron is locked in a cell here! Step on the switch in front of the cell. The door opens. Goron 1 is free!
  4. He tells you a tip: a pillar above can be knocked down to reach Darunia. He leaves a small chest behind. Open it for Small Key 1.

GORONS 2 AND 3

  1. Go back to the entry hall. Use Small Key 1 on the locked door on the east side. Go in the big room with the long bridge over lava. Fire Keese fly around. Shoot them with your Bow from far away.
  2. Go north across the big stone platforms. Some platforms sink! Move fast. A Goron is in a cage on the north side. Step on the switch to free him. Goron 2 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 2. His tip: some walls look funny. You can bomb them!
  3. Stay on the north side. Go east across more moving platforms. Watch out for Lava Bubbles that jump out of the lava. Block them with your shield. Then hit them with your Hookshot.
  4. In the northeast corner, a platform shoots up on a jet of lava. Ride it up to a high ledge. Play the Song of Time in front of the blue block. The block moves down. Go in the room behind it.
  5. Flying Tiles (floor tiles with minds of their own) fly at you! Hold up your shield. They smash into it and break. Behind them hides a Like Like (a big slug). It tries to swallow you whole! If it eats you, it can steal your Goron Tunic or your Hylian Shield! Hit it with your Hookshot to stun it. Then slash it. If it steals your gear, beat it fast and it spits your stuff back out.
  6. Beat the tiles and the Like Like. Look at the back wall. A Gold Skulltula hides there! Hit it with your Hookshot. That is Skulltula 1 of 5.
  7. Go back to the big lava room. Go to the south side. Find the cracked wall on the south edge. Throw a bomb at it! The wall blows open. A Goron is inside. Step on the switch. Goron 3 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 3. His tip: a room nearby has a platform that shoots up on a big pillar of fire.
  8. Before you leave, look at the two platforms in the lava to the southwest. The second one shoots up into the air. Jump to the hidden ledge up there. Pots hold blue and red rupees!

GORON 4 AND THE DUNGEON MAP

  1. Cross the long bridge to the east side. Use a key on the locked door. Inside, a huge jet of fire shoots up from the floor. You need something to sit on top of it. Go through the next locked door.
  2. Climb the mesh wall (the wire fence wall) to the top of the room. Keese hide in the corners. Push the big stone block on the edge so it falls onto the fire jet. Jump down and ride the block up like a lift to floor 2!
  3. Floor 2 has Torch Slugs (slow slugs that are on fire). Shoot them with your Bow from far away. Or dash in and slash them to put their fire out.
  4. Go up the big steps. A wall of fire blocks your path. Next to it is a blue switch. Hit the switch to turn it pink. The fire moves to the back wall instead!
  5. Pull the stone block out of the corner alcove. Climb up on it. Go past where the first fire wall was. Turn around and hit the switch again with your Bow or Hookshot. The fire moves again. Now you can climb up to floor 3. A Goron is locked in a cell here, but there is no switch. You must come back for him later!
  6. Floor 3 east side is a big room full of rolling boulders! Stay on the lower roads. Hug the right wall and go north. See the cracked wall? Throw a bomb at it. A Gold Skulltula hides behind it! That is Skulltula 2 of 5.
  7. Keep to the north wall past the spider. Dodge the boulders. Find an alcove with a Goron cell. Step on the switch. Goron 4 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 4. His tip: some doors in the temple are fake. They fall on you if you try to open them. Bomb them first!
  8. Weave past the boulders to the south side of the room. Find a big alcove with no cell. Inside is a big chest. Open it. It holds the Dungeon Map!

GORONS 5 AND 6 AND THE COMPASS

  1. The other cell in this room is up on the high platforms. You cannot reach it yet. Look on the west side for a locked door. Use a key and go in the hall.
  2. Inside, there is a door to the south and a sealed door to the west with an eye above it. Shoot the eye. Go through the west door first. A big chest holds the Compass!
  3. Now go through the south door. You are over a big lava pool with mesh platforms. A wall of fire starts to chase you from behind! Sprint up the walkway. Dodge the Red Bubbles. Climb up the ledge on the left. Wait for the fire wall to pass you.
  4. Jump back down and run behind the fire wall to the north side. Push the big stone block out into the last room. Now you have a shortcut. You can also reach the high platforms over the rolling boulders!
  5. Before you go up, hop north and look back south and up. See the straw on the wall? If you learned the Scarecrow's Song, play it here. Pierre the scarecrow pops up! Hookshot up to him. Jump to the small platform to the north. It rises up! Climb the mesh wall to the top floor. A Gold Skulltula hides on the wall by the door. That is Skulltula 3 of 5.
  6. In the top floor room, look along the inside wall around the pit. Another Gold Skulltula hides there! Hit it with your Hookshot. That is Skulltula 4 of 5.
  7. Step on the switch in this room. Two pillars rise up with Hookshot targets. Race along the room and Hookshot to each one before they sink down. You win three chests: two Red Rupees and one Purple Rupee. That is 90 rupees in all!
  8. Go back down to the high platforms. Jump over the rolling boulders. Beat the Fire Slugs on the way. Find a platform in the middle with a big crack. Throw a bomb at it! It blows open. A pit with a mesh wall leads down to floor 2.
  9. Climb down. You are back by the Goron cell with no switch. Now you can step on the switch! Goron 5 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 5. His tip: some switches are so rusty that even a Goron cannot push them down.
  10. Climb back up to the high platforms. Jump across the boulders to the south end of the room. Step on the switch by the last cell. Goron 6 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 6. His tip: the temple hides a big old hammer. You want it!

GORON 7 AND THE MEGATON HAMMER

  1. Go back to the room with the fire wall that chased you. Sprint east ahead of the fire wall. Go in the next area.
  2. This room is a fire trap funhouse! Hidden fire walls pop up near the thin posts. Rolling boulders roll back and forth. Move slow. Look for an opening before you step.
  3. Do NOT trust the door to the south of the first boulder. It wiggles! If you try to open it, it falls flat on your face. Throw a bomb at it instead. It is a fake door.
  4. Weave through the hidden fire walls to the north wall. A Goron is locked in a cell. Step on the switch in front of him. Goron 7 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 7. His tip: if the way is blocked, the key is "time."
  5. Go back to the south side. Find the locked door to the middle room. Inside, a switch in the middle needs something heavy to sit on it. That switch turns off the big fire jets to the west. Go east and north past a rolling boulder and two fire spouts. Find a small platform with a tiny stone block.
  6. Grab the stone. Throw it through the fires toward the switch. You do not have to carry it all the way! Go back around to the switch. Put the stone on it. All the fire traps in the room stop!
  7. Use your last key on the next door. A Flare Dancer jumps out of a fire pit! It skates around in a ring of fire. It cannot be hurt while it is on fire.
  8. Wait until it comes close. Hit it with your Hookshot! The Hookshot pulls its metal core out of the fire. The tiny body runs away. Chase it and slash it with your sword! It is fast. Run the other way to cut it off. Or throw Bomb Flowers in its path.
  9. After you hurt it, it jumps back in the fire pit. Blue flames bounce around the room. Dodge them. Pull it out with your Hookshot again. Keep going until it blows up! A platform falls onto the pit. A lift opens up to floor 4.
  10. Ride the lift up. Climb up. Fire jets block the path. A colored switch sits below. Throw a Bomb Flower down at the switch to lower the jets. Throw a second Bomb Flower. Run to the mesh wall and climb up while the jets are down!
  11. On floor 5, a rolling boulder blocks the path to the right. A switch sits in front of you. Step on it. A narrow path rises up. The fire walls drop for a short time. Run up the narrow path fast before the fires come back!
  12. At the top is a big chest. Open it. You get the Megaton Hammer! It smashes gray rocks, rusty switches, and big statues.
  13. Leave by the high ledge to the south, above the rolling boulder. At the far end is a Fairy's Thought. It says the fairy loves bright places. Play the Sun's Song. Fairy magic heals you!

GORON 8

  1. Look north for a big raised button over a grate. Hit it with your Megaton Hammer! The floor breaks open. Jump down.
  2. A big statue made of pieces blocks the next door. Swing your Hammer to the side. Smash it to bits! Go through.
  3. Hit the button in the next room. Platforms drop down. You are back on floor 3, in the middle of the big boulder room. Smash the big gray rocks with your Hammer to get into the area above the prison.
  4. Play the Song of Time in front of the blue block here. It opens a hole down to a switch. Hit the switch with your Megaton Hammer! Goron 8 is free! He leaves a small chest with Small Key 8. His tip: remember the big statue in the entry hall.

GORON 9 AND THE BOSS KEY

  1. Near where Goron 8 was locked up, there is a big panel on the wall. Hit it with your Megaton Hammer! The pillar falls down. You drop all the way to floor 1, right in front of the boss door.
  2. Go back to the entry hall. Smash the big statue with your Hammer! A locked door hides behind it. Use Small Key 8 on it.
  3. Smash all the Lava Slugs in the next room with your Hammer. The Hammer sends them flying and puts their fire out! The far door opens.
  4. The next room has Flying Tiles and a Like Like. Block the tiles with your shield first. Then stun the Like Like with your Hookshot and slash it. Or throw bombs at it from far away.
  5. Look for the big gray rock at the end of the room. Smash it with your Hammer! A Gold Skulltula hides behind it. That is Skulltula 5 of 5.
  6. Smash the big statue blocking the next door. A second Flare Dancer attacks! Pull it out of the fire with your Hookshot. Then slam it with your Megaton Hammer. The Hammer stuns it so you get free hits!
  7. This dancer is tougher. When it jumps back in the fire, green flames bounce around. There are more of them this time. Be careful. Do not chase it when the flames are near.
  8. Beat the second Flare Dancer. A small chest pops up with a Red Rupee. Go south.
  9. Here is the last Goron! He has been guarding a big blue chest since the very start. Step on the switch to free him. Goron 9 is free — all 9 Gorons are free! Open the big blue chest. It holds the Boss Key!
  10. Go back to the entry hall. Go east and cross the pillar you knocked down. Hookshot to the north side. Climb the high ledge. Pots hold hearts and magic. Catch the Fairy in a bottle if you have an empty one!
  11. Use your Boss Key on the boss door. Time to face the dragon!
BOSS: VOLVAGIA
He is a giant fire dragon! He hides in the lava holes in the floor. Stand near a hole and wait. Some holes bubble first as a fake-out — do not fall for the first bubbles! When Volvagia pops up for real, run to him fast. Jump and slam him on the head with your Megaton Hammer! Then slash his head with your sword. He flies up and breathes fire across the room. Run sideways, away from the fire trail. Do not stand in the burning spots he leaves! Later he shakes the ceiling. Rocks rain down! Hold up your shield (ZL) and walk to the side and back. Block the rocks from the front. When he pops out of a hole again, slam him with the Hammer. Do it again and again until he is done!
YOU GET: Megaton Hammer, Goron Tunic, Heart Container, Fire Medallion, Bolero of Fire
KID TIP! The Fire Temple has a lot of locked doors. If you run out of keys, you missed a Goron cell. Go back and look for cells you did not open yet! And watch out for Like Likes — they can eat your Goron Tunic. If one eats you, beat it fast to get your stuff back.
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas (5): (1) Back wall of the Like Like room past the Song of Time block. (2) Behind the cracked wall in the boulder room. (3) Top floor by the door, reached with Pierre the scarecrow (play the Scarecrow's Song). (4) On the inner wall around the pit in the top floor room. (5) Behind the gray rock in the Flying Tiles and Like Like room near the boss.
• Small Keys (8): One from each of the first 8 freed Gorons. The 9th Goron guards the Boss Key instead.
• Boss Key: Big blue chest next to the 9th Goron, behind the smashed statue in the entry hall.
• Grown-up Heart Pieces: Ride the bean plants you planted as a kid — #17 on Death Mountain Trail and #21 in the crater.
• After the temple: Smash the gray rocks in the crater to find a Great Fairy who gives you Double Magic (200 rupees), and meet Biggoron at the top of the mountain!
