⭐ BONUS: THE ICE CAVERN

Goal: Get the Iron Boots so you can walk on the lake floor in the Water Temple!

  1. Go to Zora's Domain. It is all frozen now! No water flows. The Zora Shop is frozen shut. The Zora are gone — frozen under the ice! Only King Zora is left, and he is stuck in a block of glowing red ice. Normal fire can not melt it. You need Blue Fire, and that is inside the Ice Cavern.
  2. Bring an empty bottle! Two empty bottles is even better. You will carry Blue Fire in them.
  3. Walk past King Zora up to Zora's Fountain. Lord Jabu-Jabu is gone. Icebergs cover the lake. A winding ice bridge curves up to the north. Walk it with care. It is slippery! Octoroks shoot rocks at you. Block them with your shield.
  4. On the ice bridge, look east. Small ice platforms lead across the water. Jump across them with care. On the far side is Heart Piece #27 on a big platform!
  5. Enter the Ice Cavern. The whole cave is ice. Go slow! Sharp icicles fall from the roof when you walk under them. If you walk slow, they crash down far ahead of you.
  6. Up ahead are chunks of red ice. Remember where they are. You will melt them soon! At the end of the hall is a big icy room with spike traps bouncing on the floor and 3 Freezards.
  7. Freezards are ice monsters. They slide toward you, then breathe freezing air. When they breathe, they can not turn! Stand to the side so they miss. They are weak to the Hookshot and to Din's Fire. Trick: stand so a spike trap is between you and a Freezard. Its freeze breath freezes the trap solid!
  8. Beat all 3 Freezards. A gate opens. Walk up the next hall. Watch for falling icicles again.
  9. Next room: a big spinning scythe trap made of ice blades! A gate at the far end needs 5 Silver Rupees. They are all over the room.
  10. Drop down the ledge. Run left. Slash the icy stalagmites on the ground. A Silver Rupee hides behind them! That is 1.
  11. The next 3 Silver Rupees are around the spinning scythe. Run counter-clockwise around it. Do not jump at the blades. Do not jump at the middle. Run past the blades, then move away to line up your next dash. Grab all 3.
  12. Climb the ledges on the north side. Look for the 5th Silver Rupee off the ledge. Wait until a blade passes, then jump back south to grab it. The gate opens!
  13. Before you go through, look up. You face south now. High on the wall above the stalagmites is a Gold Skulltula. Hit it with your Hookshot! That is Skulltula 1 of 3.
  14. Go north into the next room. It has a big ice sheet, stone platforms, Freezards, and Ice Keese flying above. Ice Keese freeze you if they hit you! Shoot them down with your Bow first.
  15. Hookshot the Freezard on the high platform. Jump north across the ice. Find a strange icy block backed by red ice. Slash the stalagmite. Blue Fire bursts out and melts the red ice! Little blue flames dance around. Fill your empty bottle with Blue Fire!
  16. Fill a second bottle too if you have one. Melt the red ice on a low platform to the south-east. A Blue Rupee hides in a pot there. Then go up the high path to the north-west.
  17. Hookshot or dodge past the Freezard guarding more red ice. Melt it with Blue Fire. A big chest holds the Dungeon Map!
  18. Go back to the scythe room. Head left (east) and melt the red ice blocking the hall. This path has spike traps and a Freezard. Go to the end.
  19. Three small alcoves! The north one has red ice. Melt it. Pots with rupees hide inside. There is also a Fairy's Thought here. Play the Sun's Song and a fairy heals you! The south alcove has a Blue Fire font. Fill your bottles again. The east alcove has stalagmites. Slash them. Watch out for Keese! A big chest holds the Compass.
  20. Turn around from the Compass chest. Do not run back yet! Behind a low icy column on the north side hides a Gold Skulltula. Hookshot it! That is Skulltula 2 of 3.
  21. Fill at least 3 bottles with Blue Fire if you can. Go back to the scythe room. Walk back toward the door. Melt the red ice chunks you passed at the start. Two chunks by the first Freezard fight hide pots with a Red Rupee and a fairy. The farthest chunk hides a pot with a Magic Jar.
  22. Now melt the red ice on the west path from the scythe room. Cross a small gap into a big room full of blocks and pillars. 5 more Silver Rupees hide here. Some are too high to reach. But there is a short ice block you can push!
  23. Shoot the Ice Keese first. Then push the ice block west. It slides until it hits a tall pillar. Climb up and grab Silver Rupee 1.
  24. Push the block north. It stops under a ledge full of red ice. Climb up. Melt the red ice. Silver Rupee 2!
  25. Push the block to the side so it slides into a pit. A new block pops back at the start. Push it south to reach a tall pillar. Climb up for Silver Rupee 3.
  26. Push the block west again. It hits one more tall pillar. Silver Rupee 4 is on top. Silver Rupee 5 is on a low platform just to the right.
  27. Look around this room. There is a Blue Fire font in the west corner. In the north-west corner is a ledge with a Triforce mark. Push the block north, west, south, then west again. It lands under the Blue Fire font. Climb up and fill a bottle!
  28. Play the Song of Time at the Triforce mark. Blue blocks appear! Walk across them to the north-west ledge. Open the small chest for a Red Rupee.
  29. To reach the gate, reset the block into a pit. Push the new block south, west, north, east, then south. Climb up and go down the south hall.
  30. Beat the Freezards. Melt the last red ice blocking the door. If you are out of Blue Fire, there is one more font in a high alcove to the east.
  31. Last room! The mini-boss: White Wolfos. He is a big white wolf. His claws freeze you! Keep your shield up. Wait for him to swing twice. He swings wild and spins around. His back is open! Hit his back with your sword. Then back away and wait for the next swing. Be patient. He falls!
  32. Open the chest. The Iron Boots are yours! Put them on with D-pad Down. They make you sink to the bottom of lakes. You walk slow, but you can stand firm in wind and on shaky ground too.
  33. Sheik appears! Sheik tells you Ruto went on to the Water Temple. Sheik teaches you the Serenade of Water. It warps you to Lake Hylia!
  34. Before you leave, grab one more bottle of Blue Fire. Jump down into the pool at the south end. Walk out the tunnel with your Iron Boots on. You are back at Zora's Fountain!
  35. Go back into Zora's Domain. Walk up to King Zora. Pour Blue Fire on his red ice. It melts! He is free! He thanks you and gives you the Zora Tunic. It lets you breathe under water as long as you wear it. Now you are ready for the Water Temple!
  36. Try your new gear! Put on the Iron Boots AND the Zora Tunic. Walk into the big pool at Zora's Fountain. Sink to the bottom. There is a ring of shiny rupees around Heart Piece #28!
KID TIP! Blue Fire goes out fast! Carry it in a bottle and use it right away. Keep 2 bottles of Blue Fire on you at all times in this cave. And the Ice Cavern has 3 Gold Skulltulas — grab them while you are here! One more tip: without the Zora Tunic, walking under water hurts you. You run out of air! Always wear the blue tunic with the Iron Boots.
YOU GET: Iron Boots, Zora Tunic, Serenade of Water, Dungeon Map, Compass
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas: 3 in the Ice Cavern — the scythe room high wall, behind the low icy column by the Compass chest, and the high wall across the gap in the ice block room.
• Heart Pieces #27 and #28: #27 is on the ice platforms on the way to the cave. #28 is at the bottom of Zora's Fountain — wear the Iron Boots and the Zora Tunic!
