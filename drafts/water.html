10. WATER TEMPLE

The hardest temple in the game — take your time!

Goal: Wake Ruto, the Sage of Water. This is the hardest temple — take your time!

Before you go in, you need the Iron Boots and the Zora Tunic from the Ice Cavern chapter just before this one! The Iron Boots let you walk on the lake floor. The Zora Tunic lets you breathe under water.

THE WATER LEVELS

The water in this temple sits at one of three heights: low (floor 1), middle (floor 2), or high (floor 3). Each height opens new doors and floods others. To change the water, find a wall with the royal Triforce mark and play Zelda's Lullaby there.

New in the remake: press down on the D-pad to swap your Iron Boots on and off fast. Boots on = you sink and walk on the floor. Boots off = you swim and float up. You will swap them a lot!

Your map shows which floors are under water. Once you have the Compass, the map also marks the spots where you can change the water level!

KID TIP! Stuck in the Water Temple? Check the water level! Most stuck spots are fixed by changing the water to low, middle, or high. And wear the Iron Boots to sink, then take them off to float back up.

GETTING THE DUNGEON MAP

  1. Play the Serenade of Water to warp to Lake Hylia. Put on your Iron Boots to walk on the lake floor. Hookshot the target in the dry lake bed to open the temple door. Go in!
  2. Take off your Iron Boots and swim up to the platform above. You are in the big main room. A tall tower stands in the middle. The water is all the way up to the top floor (floor 3).
  3. Swim to the tower. Most doors here are too far to reach. Look east. A gate blocks a chest and a wall with a royal mark. Put on your Iron Boots and sink all the way down to floor 1.
  4. Watch out for Spikes (rolling balls with metal spikes). Hold up your shield! Their spikes bounce off your shield. Then hit them with your Hookshot.
  5. Find a doorway with two glowing blue pillars. Above it is a picture of three floors with only the bottom one lit up. That means this room changes the water level. Go in!
  6. Princess Ruto is here! She is looking for the monster in this temple too. She swims off and leaves it to you.
  7. Take off your Iron Boots. Float up to the top floor. A big chest holds the Dungeon Map! You saw it behind the bars from the main room.
  8. Look at the wall nearby. It has the royal Triforce mark. Play Zelda's Lullaby. The water drops all the way down to floor 1!

GETTING THE COMPASS

  1. With the water low, hop down into the small pool on floor 1. You see two unlit torches and one lit torch. Shoot an arrow through the lit torch flame to light the other two torches. Or use Din's Fire! The sealed door to the east opens.
  2. Go in. The door locks! Three Spikes attack. Block their spikes with your shield. Slash them when they pull their spikes back in. Beat all three. A small chest pops up with Small Key 1.
  3. Look for a cracked wall on the south side of the tower. Blow it up with a bomb! Go down the path to the west. Put on your Iron Boots and sink to floor B1 (the basement).
  4. A Shell Blade (an angry clam) attacks. Block its charge with your shield. Wait for the clam to open wide. Then hit its soft inside with your Hookshot!
  5. Swim up past the Shell Blade. Climb some steps to a switch. Hit it to raise a statue with a Hookshot target. Hookshot across the gap.
  6. A Like Like waits above! Shoot it with your Bow from far away. Do NOT let it eat you. If it eats your Zora Tunic, you cannot buy a new one — the Zora shop is closed! If it does eat you, kill it fast to get your stuff back.
  7. Beat the Like Like. A big chest pops up. Open it for the Compass!

RAISING THE WATER TO 2F (MIDDLE)

  1. Go back to the main room with the tower. Go west of the tower. Push the big stone block forward until it falls into the pool below.
  2. Put on your Iron Boots and drop in after it. Beat another Shell Blade. Swim up to the opening to the north. A small chest sits on a high ledge. You cannot reach it yet. Remember it!
  3. Jump in the water. A big whirlpool spins you around! Sink down with your Iron Boots. Land on the narrow snake-like platform next to a snake statue. The snake's mouth holds a switch. Hit it with your Hookshot. A gate under the water opens.
  4. Swap your boots on and off to float through the gate. Keep your boots on past the gate. Two more Shell Blades attack. Beat them. Swim up the far side. A platform holds a chest with Small Key 2!
  5. Now open the locked door with green pillars at the base of the tower. Go in. Look up. Hookshot to the target above. Ride up to a ledge with another Triforce mark. Play Zelda's Lullaby. The water rises to floor 2 (middle)!

RAISING THE WATER TO 3F (HIGH)

  1. With the water at middle level, a floating block inside the tower has risen up. It leaves a hole down to the basement. Put on your Iron Boots and sink to the bottom. Beat three Spikes.
  2. Hit the green switch above you with your Hookshot. A gate opens on a low ceiling. A Shell Blade drops down. Beat it. Swim up through the gate to a small underwater room. A small chest holds a Red Rupee.
  3. Look around the walls of this little room. A Gold Skulltula hides here! Hit it with your Hookshot. That is Skulltula 1 of 5.
  4. Swim back up the tower and out. (A spider hides in the tower too. You cannot reach it yet. Come back later with the Longshot!)
  5. Swim east to where you met Ruto. Sink to the bottom with your Iron Boots. Halfway up the wall is a cracked wall, now high and dry. Plant a bomb on the ledge. It blows open!
  6. Go in. Beat the Shell Blades with your sword. Look at the wall behind you. A Gold Skulltula hides there! That is Skulltula 2 of 5.
  7. Swim back up to the water line east of the tower. Find another alcove with a cracked wall. Bomb it open. Go northeast. Put on your Iron Boots and sink down. Stingers (flying fish) swarm you under water. Block their dives with your shield. Then hit them with your Hookshot.
  8. Take off your boots at the far end. A big geyser shoots up to an alcove. Hit the switch in the small pool with your Hookshot to lower the geyser. Step on it. Hit the switch again to ride up! The alcove holds a small chest with Small Key 3.
  9. Swim to the west side of the tower. Floating blocks now line up to a locked door with red pillars. Go in. Another geyser blocks the way. Hit the switch to lower it. A Tektite drops down. Beat it. Stand on the platform. Hit the switch again to ride up to floor 3.
  10. You are in front of the last Triforce mark. Play Zelda's Lullaby. The water rises to floor 3 (high)!

DARK LINK AND THE LONGSHOT

  1. Hop down to the tower platform. Look back at the ledge you jumped from. See the straw up there? Play the Scarecrow's Song. Pierre pops up! Now you can Hookshot back up here fast any time you need to change the water.
  2. Go west to the second locked door on the floating platforms. Inside is a long room with a pit. Moving platforms on the far side slide toward the middle and sink. Beat the Keese first.
  3. Slide down to a moving platform. It goes up and down. Aim at the targets on the far platforms and Hookshot across. Move fast before they drop! Rest on the still platform to the side if you need a break. There is a Fairy's Thought here. It wants sunbeams to beat the wet air. Play the Sun's Song. Fairy magic heals you!
  4. Wait for the highest platform to come close. Hookshot to it. Then zip up to the ledge above to get out of the pit.
  5. The next room has high platforms over a pool full of Tektites. Shoot the Tektites with your Bow from far away first.
  6. Hit the blue switch in the middle. Statues rise up with Hookshot targets. Hookshot to the west one. Do NOT Hookshot the north statue next, or you will fall in the pit! Hit the switch again to lower the statue. Hookshot past it to the left.
  7. Hit the switch again to raise the statues. Hookshot to the northeast one. It leads to a high ledge. Hit the switch two more times to lower the statue. Jump on top of it. Ride it up! Break the pots for items. Go in the door.
  8. This room looks like a big shallow lake with one tree on a little island. Walk to the far side. The way is blocked. Turn around. A dark figure waits by the tree. It is YOU! Dark Link — a shadow copy of you!
MINI-BOSS: DARK LINK
He copies your moves! He has his own Master Sword and shield. Do not just run at him. He blocks your sword and can even stand on your sword! Wait for him to attack. He swings three times. The last swing has a red trail. Dodge that last swing! Then he is open. Run in and hit him. Your Bow and Hookshot do not work well on him. Din's Fire works at the start — it hurts him and stuns him! But after he fades away and comes back, he gets smarter. He backflips away from slow attacks. Later he stabs twice, then jumps at you. Dodge to the side when he leaps! He may fake you out and wait before he jumps. He may also roll at you and spin. Backflip away from his spin! When he starts his spin, run in fast and jump-slash him. Keep hitting him after his big moves. When you deal enough damage, he is gone for good!
  1. After Dark Link falls, go through the door. Open the big chest. You get the Longshot! It reaches twice as far as your Hookshot.

GETTING THE BOSS KEY

  1. Step on the platform behind the chest. Try out your Longshot on the far targets above. Zip up to a high ledge. A waterfall pit leads down to a river cave. This cave goes all the way back to the temple entrance!
  2. Move along the river. Whirlpools suck you into pits! Wear your Iron Boots to hold your ground. Climb up on ledges to go around the pits.
  3. When the path turns south, look behind you. A Gold Skulltula sits high on a cave pillar. Hit it with your Longshot while you wear your Iron Boots. That is Skulltula 3 of 5.
  4. Go through the last two whirlpools. Walk through the middle to the southeast corner. Look for ledges after the whirlpool. A pot holds a Fairy! Catch it in a bottle if you have an empty one.
  5. Stay on the ledge. Find a big gate with an eye target. Shoot the eye. Longshot across fast before the gate closes! Go out to the main room.
  6. The water is still at high level. Sink to the bottom. Go back to where you met Ruto. Play Zelda's Lullaby at the Triforce mark to lower the water to floor 1 (low).
  7. Climb back inside the tower. Play Zelda's Lullaby to raise the water. Look up! With your Longshot you can reach a high target at the very top of the tower. Zip up to a high platform. A Gold Skulltula hides on the other side of the ceiling. That is Skulltula 4 of 5.
  8. With the water at middle level, go out on the tower balcony to the north side. Shoot the eye target on the far wall. It shows a far target your Longshot can reach. Zip across to the north room.
  9. You see a small chest behind a fence. Jump in the pool to the left. Sink with your Iron Boots. A big stone block sits in a small opening. Pull it all the way back.
  10. Get out of the pool. Look right of the fence. A high waterfall alcove has a target on the ceiling. Longshot to it! You drop down on the other side of the water. Push the stone block north. Now you can reach the chest. Open it for Small Key 4!
  11. Go back to the tower room. Sink all the way to the bottom on the north side. Drop down into a basement pit going north. Swim up to the ledge. Longshot across to the far door. Use Small Key 4 to open it.
  12. The big room past it has a pool full of Tektites. Boulders roll out of side alcoves and into two whirlpool pits. Shoot the Tektites with your Bow first!
  13. Wait for a boulder to pass. Sink with your Iron Boots. Take them off to cross a ledge. Put them back on. Stop before the next boulder path. Wait for the second boulder to pass. Swim up to the ledge. Dodge the whirlpools!
  14. The next room has a small pool full of Stingers and three cracked walls on the left. Throw bombs in the water to kill the Stingers. Block any that fly up with your shield.
  15. Bomb all three walls. The middle wall has a big stone block. The side walls let you push the block from behind. Push it into the pool onto a switch! The water rises up to a high ledge.
  16. Go through to the next room. Step on a switch to raise geyser platforms. Tektites may bother you. Shoot them with your Bow. Cross to the far side.
  17. You see a big blue chest behind a gate. Go through the door nearby. You are in the waterway where the boulders roll. Wait for a boulder to pass. Dash east, then north to an alcove with a door. A Shell Blade guards it. Beat it. Go in the next room. Open the chest. You get the Boss Key!
  18. On your way back through the boulder room, look up. A small waterfall comes from above. A Gold Skulltula hides up there, across from the falls. Hit it fast with your Longshot before the next boulder rolls by. That is Skulltula 5 of 5!

TO THE BOSS DOOR

  1. Go back to the tower room. Use Pierre the scarecrow to get up to the floor 3 ledge fast. Play Zelda's Lullaby to raise the water to floor 3 (high).
  2. Go to the north side of the tower. Use your Longshot on the far target to reach the high ledge. Walk toward the boss door.
  3. The road is a long slope with water rushing down. Spike traps bounce from side to side! Put on your Iron Boots to get a good grip. Walk up slow. Go around the spikes. At the top is the boss door. Use your Boss Key. Get ready!
BOSS: MORPHA
She is a giant water blob! Her real body is a little ball hiding inside the goo. Stay on the platforms. Do not swim in the water — you cannot hurt her there. When she pops up, she slams down to crush you. She slams up to three times in a row! Move away fast each time. After her slams, she rests for a moment. Hit her with your Longshot! The Longshot pulls the little ball out of the water. Then slash the ball with your sword! When she gets hurt, she jumps back in the water and spins around in a big circle. Hookshot to a target on the wall to zip away to safety! If she grabs you, she wraps around you and hurts you a lot. Mash your buttons fast to break free! Later she makes lots of extra arms in the water. They all slam down too. Find the real one with the ball inside. Pull it out with your Longshot and slash it. Do it again and again until she is gone!
YOU GET: Longshot, Heart Container, Water Medallion, Serenade of Water
KID TIP! The Iron Boots come from the Ice Cavern, NOT from this temple. If you do not have them yet, go back and do the Ice Cavern chapter first! Also: your map shows which floors are under water. If a door looks too high or too low, change the water level first.
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas (5): (1) Small underwater room in the basement below the tower. (2) Behind the cracked wall east of Ruto's room (Shell Blade room). (3) High on a pillar in the underground river cave after Dark Link. (4) At the very top of the tower, reached with the Longshot. (5) Above the waterfall in the rolling-boulder room near the Boss Key.
• Small Keys (4): The remake has 4 Small Keys, not 6 like the old game. (1) Torch room after lowering the water. (2) Whirlpool room with the snake statue. (3) Geyser room on the east side. (4) Behind the stone block in the north room, after the Longshot.
• Boss Key: In the room past the rolling-boulder waterway, guarded by a Shell Blade.
• After the boss: The ice melts and Lake Hylia fills back up! Go outside at sunrise. Aim your Bow at the rising sun and shoot. Light falls to the earth — the Fire Arrows land on a small island! Swim over and grab them. Then play the Sun's Song to make it night. Longshot the vines on top of the island tree. A Gold Skulltula hides up there!
• Grown-up Heart Pieces: #27 and #28 at Zora's Fountain (iceberg + underwater cave), #30 and #31 at Lake Hylia (lab diving with the Golden Scale + the bean tower).
