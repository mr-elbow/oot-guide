⭐ BONUS: BOTTOM OF THE WELL

Goal: Get the Lens of Truth — it shows you things that are invisible! You need it for the Shadow Temple.

  1. First, as a grown-up, finish the Water Temple. Look across Hyrule Field. Smoke and fire rise over Kakariko Village! Ride Epona there fast!
  2. In the village, Sheik stands alone by the well. A creature of darkness bursts out! It knocks Link down and escapes into the Shadow Temple. When Link wakes up, Sheik tells the tale. Long ago, Impa sealed a dark beast under the well. It broke free. Impa went into the Shadow Temple to stop it.
  3. Sheik teaches you the Nocturne of Shadow. It warps you to the graveyard. Sheik warns you: the Shadow Temple is full of traps you can only see with the Eyes of Truth. But Sheik does not know where to find them.
  4. Try the Shadow Temple door yourself. It is sealed. A strange plaque stands in an empty room. You need the Lens first!
  5. Talk to the old man in the village. He tells you about a person who lived here long ago. That person had the "eyes of truth". Their house stood right where the well is now!
  6. Climb down into the well. At the bottom is a sealed door. There is also a tiny crawl space. It is too small for a grown-up. You must go back to being a kid!
  7. Before you leave, grab a treat. Hookshot onto the vines on the low roof of Granny's Potion Shop. Look up at the windmill. There is a high opening with a ladder. Use the Longshot to zip to the ladder. Climb to the top of the windmill. A chest holds a Purple Rupee worth 50!
  8. Warp to the Temple of Time with the Prelude of Light. Put the Master Sword back in its stone. You are a kid again!
  9. Go to the windmill in Kakariko Village. The windmill man is still there. Remember his strange tune? He is mad because a boy played a weird song here 7 years ago. That boy was you!
  10. Take out your ocarina. The windmill man will repeat the song the boy played. Play it back. You learn the Song of Storms! It makes rain and wind. Now play it here in the windmill. The windmill spins out of control! The well outside drains dry!
  11. Climb down into the Bottom of the Well. It is dark and creepy. Bring bombs, your Boomerang, and your Kokiri Sword. Watch out for the hands on the floor — they grab you!
  12. Walk down a few halls. Skulltulas guard the way. At the end is a blank wall with a plaque. It says: "A wall is not merely a wall. Do not accept only what you see as truth." Walk straight into the wall. It is fake! You pass right through.
  13. You are in the main room. Four long halls form a square around a big closed room in the middle. A Green Bubble flies around the halls, counter-clockwise. Its green flames hurt you. But when it turns a corner, the flames go out for a moment! Run up from behind and hit it. Do this a few times to beat it for good.
  14. Watch out for hidden pits in the floor! Some floors are fake. If you fall in, you drop to the basement floor below, called B2. It is full of water and Redeads! Play the Sun's Song to freeze the Redeads, then beat them.
  15. In B2, you can not leave until you find 5 Silver Rupees. Here they are: 2 across the thin plants floating in the green ooze (the ooze hurts, so step with care). 1 in a pool of water to the north-west. 1 on a dry path in front of some boulders to the north. 1 past the boulders in the south-east corner — bomb the boulders to reach it! A Redead guards a chest there with the Dungeon Map inside.
  16. With the map and a way out, climb the ladder back up to the west side. Go to the north side of the main halls. You will see fountain heads feeding the water. In front of the biggest one is a Triforce mark.
  17. Stand on the Triforce mark and play Zelda's Lullaby. The fountains stop! The water in the halls drops. A new area opens to the north. Crawl through the tunnel.
  18. Beat the Skulltula in the hall. At the end is a small chest. But before you reach it, you fall into a pit! You drop into a new part of the basement. The boss of this mini-dungeon waits here: Dead Hand.
  19. Dead Hand is gross and scary. Long hands pop out of the ground. If one grabs you, it holds you tight. Mash the buttons to break free! Then Dead Hand's head pops up to bite you. That is your chance! Slash his face with your sword. He takes damage ONLY in the face.
  20. After a few hits, Dead Hand burrows away. More hands pop up all over the room. More hands means more chances to lure him out — but they also grab you more. Trick: you do not have to get grabbed! Cut all the hands with your sword, or hit lots of hands at once with a bomb or Din's Fire. That lures him out too!
  21. Keep luring him out. Wait until he lowers his head. Slash his face. Or blast him with Din's Fire. Soon he falls! In his place is a chest. Open it. The Lens of Truth is yours!
  22. Put the Lens on with D-pad Up, like a mask. Now you can see the unseen! Fake walls fade away. Hidden pits show up. Invisible chests and bad guys appear! But the Lens slowly uses your magic. Do not leave it on all the time.
  23. Try it out! Look at the south edge of the pit. A hidden ladder appears. Climb up. This hall has secret alcoves the Lens shows you. Pots hide inside. The path ends on a secret ledge above the hall you came in by.
  24. Look around on the mesh platform with the Lens on. A small chest hides there with a Purple Rupee! Then go back to the small chest that lured you into Dead Hand's pit. With the Lens on, jump over the pit. Open it for a Business Scrub Seed.
  25. Go back to the four halls. Use the Lens to find a new opening into the big closed room in the middle. Inside, the Lens shows many pits — even around the torture rack in the center and in the two cells on the south side. A big gap blocks the big chest.
  26. Grab the small chest with a Blue Rupee (a Skulltula guards it). Then look behind the big chest to the west. The Lens shows an opening! Go back out to the west hall. Jump across from the mesh platforms. You find the Compass!
  27. Now hunt the rest. In the south-west corner, a hidden ledge leads to a Deku Business Scrub. He sells Green Potions (good for long Lens use!) once you beat him up.
  28. In the north-west alcove, beat the Skulltula. Use the Lens to dodge the pits. A small chest holds a Red Rupee.
  29. In the north-east corner, use the Lens near the plaque to dodge pits by the east door. Inside are Fire Keese and a Beamos. A small chest in the corner holds bombs. Watch the floor — pits everywhere!
  30. Go south into the next room. A Like Like waits in the bright room. It is a tall sack monster. If it grabs you, it eats your Deku Shield! Keep your distance. Stun it with your Boomerang first. Then run in, slash it, and back off fast. Repeat until it falls. If it eats your shield, beat it and pick your shield back up.
  31. After the Like Like falls, look up on the wall between the two torches. A Gold Skulltula hides there! That is Skulltula 1 of 3.
  32. Leave by the ladder back to the east hall. Drop down into the dry waterways under the grating. A small chest holds a Blue Rupee. Walk the south hall. Find a small drain hole on the south-west side. Crawl through it for another chest with a Blue Rupee.
  33. Climb back up. Go to the east side of the south hall. Look north with the Lens on. Left of the door to the middle room is a small opening in the wall. Jump across to it. A small chest holds a Red Rupee.
  34. Go to the south-east corner. Use the Lens to look at the middle room. Jump through the opening. A small chest holds the first Small Key.
  35. Jump back into the middle room. Open the locked door in the north-east corner. It is a pit room with Keese flying around. The Lens shows a path around the edge. Follow it to the second Gold Skulltula!
  36. For the second key, go to the west side of the dungeon. Jump across the drained hall from the middle room. The water is low enough to dive down and swim to the other side. Climb the mesh walls.
  37. Last room! It is full of coffins covered in webs. Burn the webs with a Deku Stick or Din's Fire. Some coffins hide Keese and pots. Some hide Gibdos — mummies like strong Redeads. Burn them with Din's Fire! One coffin has a Fairy's Thought. Play the Song of Storms to get healed.
  38. Burn the lone coffin on the east side. Inside is the second Small Key!
  39. Go back to the middle room. Open the locked door in the north-west corner. A Deku Baba guards a small room. In the corner is the third and last Gold Skulltula!
  40. You are done! Before you grow up again, use your Lens for one sweet prize. Go to Hyrule Castle Town at night. Play the Treasure Box Shop game. It costs 10 rupees. Each room has 2 chests. Wear the Lens! You can see what is inside each chest. Pick the Small Key chest every time. Win all 5 rooms to reach the prize room. The chest there holds Heart Piece #6!
  41. When you are ready, take the Master Sword again to grow up. Warp to Kakariko Graveyard. The Shadow Temple waits!
KID TIP! The Well has 3 Gold Skulltulas as a kid — grab them while you are here! Two of them can only be seen through the Lens of Truth. And remember: the Lens uses magic. If your magic runs out, look for green magic jars. The Business Scrub in the south-west corner sells Green Potions!
YOU GET: Lens of Truth, Song of Storms, Nocturne of Shadow, Dungeon Map, Compass
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas: 3 in the Bottom of the Well — the Like Like room wall, the north-east locked room edge path, and the north-west locked room corner. The last two need the Lens of Truth!
• Heart Piece #6: Win the Treasure Box Shop in Castle Town with your new Lens (10 rupees, pick the key chest in all 5 rooms).
• Watch out: Like Likes eat your Deku Shield! Stun them with the Boomerang first, and always grab your shield back after the fight.
