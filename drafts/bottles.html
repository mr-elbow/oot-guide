🧴 BOTTLES & UPGRADES

ALL 5 BOTTLES (the remake has 5 — the old game had 4!)

Bottles hold potions, fairies, bugs, fish, and milk. Fairies in bottles save your life if you fall in a fight!

  • 1. Potion Shop Bottle 👦 NEW in the remake! The first time you walk into Hyrule Castle Town, the Potion Shop is in a panic — a bug got out! Go in and the shop man gives you a bottle to catch the bug. You keep the bottle.
  • 2. Cucco Lady's Bottle 👦 In Kakariko Village, find Anju (the Cucco Lady) by the graveyard. Catch her 7 runaway Cuccos and put them back in the pen. She gives you an Empty Bottle!
  • 3. Super Cucco Bottle 👦 First wake Talon: get the Weird Egg from Malon at Lon Lon Ranch. Wait for it to hatch into the Wake-Up Cucco, then hold it next to sleeping Talon by the crates at Hyrule Castle. He goes home. Then go to Lon Lon Ranch, pay Talon 10 rupees, and find all 3 Super Cuccos in 30 seconds. Trick: throw all the normal Cuccos behind Talon's desk first — then only the Super Cuccos are left out in the open! You win a bottle of Lon Lon Milk. Drink it (2 sips) to keep the empty bottle.
  • 4. Ruto's Letter Bottle 👦 You need this one to finish the story! Win the Diving Game in Zora's Domain, swim through the tunnel to Lake Hylia, and dive to the lake floor to find the bottle with Ruto's Letter. Show it to King Zora. You keep the empty bottle.
  • 5. Poe Collector's Bottle 🧑 As a grown-up with Epona and the Bow, ride around Hyrule Field at night and shoot the 10 Big Poes (2 arrows each). Catch their souls in a bottle. Sell them to the Poe Collector in the Ghost Shop in the ruined castle town. In the remake, you get the bottle after 5 Poes (500 points). All 10 Poes (1,000 points) earns a Gold Rupee instead!

✨ MAGIC SPELLS — THE 3 GODDESS SPELLS

Great Fairies give you magic spells. Each one lives in a Great Fairy Fountain. To wake a fairy, stand on the Triforce mark on the floor and play Zelda's Lullaby. Then pay her what she asks. In the remake, fairies are NOT free! Bring rupees.

  • 🔥 Din's Fire — 25 rupees 👦🧑 Go to the top of Death Mountain Trail, past Goron City. The owl waits at the top. Bomb the cracked wall to the left of the owl. Go inside. Play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce mark. Pay 25 rupees. The fairy gives you Din's Fire AND your Magic Meter! Din's Fire makes a big ring of fire burst out all around you. It burns bad guys close to you. It lights torches you can not reach. You NEED it to light the torches that open the Shadow Temple. Get this one first — right after Dodongo's Cavern!
  • 💨 Farore's Wind — 100 rupees 👦🧑 Go to Zora's Fountain (past Zora's Domain, where Lord Jabu-Jabu used to be). Go to the south side. Bomb the cracked wall by the big cave opening. Go inside. Play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce mark. Pay 100 rupees. The fairy gives you Farore's Wind! This spell drops a magic mark where you stand in a dungeon. Cast it again later and — ZOOM — you jump right back to that mark. Great when you need to leave a dungeon and come back!
  • 💎 Nayru's Love — 300 rupees 🧑 Go to the Desert Colossus (past the Haunted Wasteland, by the Spirit Temple). Look for the cracked wall on the right side of the desert, between two palm trees. Bomb it open. Go inside. Play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce mark. Pay 300 rupees. The fairy gives you Nayru's Love! This spell wraps you in a blue crystal shield. No bad guy can hurt you while it lasts. Great for big boss fights!
KID TIP! Din's Fire moved in the remake! In the old game it came from the fairy by Hyrule Castle. In the remake it comes from the fairy on top of Death Mountain — and the Hyrule Castle fairy gives you a stronger Spin Attack instead (50 rupees). All 3 spells use magic. Each one costs 2 bars of your magic meter. If you run out of magic, look for green magic jars!

UPGRADES — MAKE LINK STRONGER

  • Wallets: You start holding 200 rupees. Adult's Wallet (500) = 10 Skulltula tokens. Giant's Wallet (1,000) = 30 Skulltula tokens.
  • Quivers (arrows): 30 come with the Bow. Big Quiver (40) = win the Kakariko Shooting Gallery as a grown-up. Biggest Quiver (50) = score 1,500 at Horseback Archery in Gerudo's Fortress.
  • Bomb Bags: 20 come from Dodongo's Cavern. Bigger Bomb Bag (30) = stop the rolling Goron in Goron City with a bomb (kid). Biggest Bomb Bag (40) = 2nd win at Bombchu Bowling.
  • Magic Meter + Din's Fire: Great Fairy on Death Mountain peak — bomb the wall by the owl, then play Zelda's Lullaby. Costs 25 rupees.
  • Stronger Spin Attack: Great Fairy by Hyrule Castle — climb the vines past the castle gate, bomb the rock to the right, crawl through, then play Zelda's Lullaby. Costs 50 rupees.
  • Farore's Wind: Great Fairy at Zora's Fountain — bomb the cracked wall behind Lord Jabu-Jabu, then play Zelda's Lullaby. Costs 100 rupees.
  • Double Magic: Great Fairy of Wisdom, Death Mountain Crater (grown-up) — smash the rocks at the door with the Megaton Hammer, then play Zelda's Lullaby. Costs 200 rupees.
  • Nayru's Love: Great Fairy at the Desert Colossus — bomb the cracked wall between the palm trees, then play Zelda's Lullaby. Costs 300 rupees.
  • Double Defense: Great Fairy of Courage, outside Ganon's Castle (grown-up) — move the big rock with the Golden Gauntlets. All hits hurt half as much! Costs 500 rupees.
  • Silver Scale: Win the Diving Game in Zora's Domain (kid). Dive to 6 meters.
  • Golden Scale: Fishing Pond at Lake Hylia (grown-up). Catch a huge fish (about 14 pounds!) and show it to the pond man. Dive to 9 meters.
  • Goron's Bracelet: Darunia gives it to you after you cheer him up with Saria's Song. Lets you pick up Bomb Flowers.
  • Silver Gauntlets: Spirit Temple, kid side. Push huge blocks!
  • Golden Gauntlets: Ganon's Castle. Lift the giant rocks!
KID TIP! Great Fairies want rupees for their gifts in the remake (25 to 500 rupees). All six fountains cost 1,175 rupees in all. Bring cash when you visit a fairy fountain! Visit the Death Mountain fairy first — it gives you the Magic Meter and Din's Fire, and you need both for the rest of the game.
