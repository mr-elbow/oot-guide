⭐ BONUS: GET EPONA (YOUR HORSE!)

Goal: Get your very own horse. Epona makes crossing Hyrule Field fast and fun!

  1. First, as a kid, go to Lon Lon Ranch and talk to Malon in the horse field. She teaches you Epona's Song. Play it near the horses — Epona will come to you! (See the Kakariko chapter for the full steps.) You must learn this song as a kid. You can not learn it as a grown-up!
  2. As a grown-up, go back to the ranch. Things are bad! The mean stable hand Ingo has taken over. He calls it Ingo Ranch now. He makes Malon work for him. He sells the horses to help Ganondorf!
  3. At night, Malon stands alone at the edge of the field, singing softly. She wishes someone would take Epona away from this place. That someone is you!
  4. During the day, talk to Ingo. Pay him 20 rupees to ride a horse in the pen. (In the old game it was 10. In the remake it is 20!)
  5. Once you are inside the pen, play Epona's Song on your ocarina. Epona is all grown up now. She comes galloping over to you! Get on her with A.
  6. Take a minute to learn to ride. Press A to use a carrot. Carrots make Epona run faster. You have 6 carrots. They come back after a short rest. Practice turning corners. Practice jumping over the low fences in the pen.
  7. When you feel ready, ride up to Ingo while you are on Epona. Talk to him. He bets you a race! One lap around the track. If you win, you get 20 rupees. If he wins, he keeps your money.
  8. Race 1: Stay on the inside of the track. That is the short way around. Save your carrots for the straight parts. Use one carrot, wait a bit, then use the next. Do not use them all at once! Gallop past Ingo on the straights. Win the race!
  9. Ingo is furious! He bets you again. This time, if you win, you keep Epona! If he wins, he keeps the money. He is faster this time. But you can still beat him.
  10. Race 2: Same plan, but faster. Use carrots on the straights when Ingo tries to pass you. Beat him around the corners on the inside. On the last straight, use all your carrots left and blaze over the finish line!
  11. You won Epona! But Ingo locks the gate. He will not let you leave. Look around the ranch walls. There are low fences on each side. Pick one with a long run-up.
  12. Ride Epona back as far as you can. Turn her toward the fence. Use carrots to build up speed. When you reach the fence, press X to jump. Epona leaps right over it! Epona is yours forever!
  13. Now you can call Epona any time in Hyrule Field or Lake Hylia. Just play Epona's Song. She comes galloping to you!
  14. Go back to the ranch later. Ingo has come to his senses. Talon is back in charge! Malon thanks you. She offers a new game: an obstacle course race. Ride around the track, jump the fences, dodge the blocks. Finish in under 40 seconds and she sends a prize to your house in Kokiri Forest: a whole cow! Play Epona's Song for the cow any time to get fresh Lon Lon Milk.
KID TIP! With Epona you can jump the fence, win Horseback Archery (Heart Piece #35!), and chase the Big Poes at night (Bottle #5). She is the best! And save your carrots. A carrot used on a turn is wasted. A carrot used on a straight wins races.
YOU GET: Epona the horse, Epona's Song (learned as a kid)
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Trading quest: After you get Epona, go to Kakariko. The Pocket Cucco Anju gave you has hatched by now. Wake up Talon with it in the big house by the village center. He hurries back to the ranch — and Anju gives you Cojiro, the next step to the Biggoron's Sword!
• Scarecrow Song: Ride Epona to Lake Hylia. Bonooru the scarecrow is still there. Play your Scarecrow's Song for him again as a grown-up. Now Pierre will pop up on high spots when you play it — and you can Hookshot to him!
