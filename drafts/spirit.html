12. SPIRIT TEMPLE

A desert temple you explore as both a kid and a grown-up.

Goal: Wake Nabooru, the Sage of Spirit. You need BOTH Young Link and Adult Link for this one!

GETTING THERE

  1. First, you need the Gerudo Pass. Free the four carpenters locked up in Gerudo's Fortress. Then you can cross into the desert.
  2. Cross the Haunted Wasteland west of Gerudo Valley. Follow the flags! Use your Lens of Truth to see the invisible guide through the sandstorm. Follow the Poe to the Desert Colossus.
  3. Go to the Spirit Temple door as a grown-up. Sheik meets you outside and teaches you the Requiem of Spirit.
  4. Now go back to the Temple of Time. Put the Master Sword back in its stone. You turn back into a kid! Play the Requiem of Spirit to warp back to the desert.
  5. Before you go in as a kid, look left of the temple door. Soft dirt sits there. Drop a bug from a bottle on it. A Gold Skulltula pops out! Then plant a Magic Bean in the dirt. You will ride the big plant as a grown-up.
KID TIP! Bring the right gear! As a kid, bring your Boomerang, Slingshot, bombs, and Deku Sticks. As a grown-up, bring your Longshot, Bow, Megaton Hammer, bombs, and Lens of Truth. You also need Zelda's Lullaby and the Song of Time.

THE KID SIDE

  1. Go in as Young Link. Walk left up the stairs. Talk to Nabooru. She is locked out of her own temple! She asks you to go in the small hole in the wall and find the Silver Gauntlets for her. Crawl through the hole.
  2. Beat the bad guys in the next room. Go through the door on the right. Stand between the two braziers (the fire bowls). Light them both with Din's Fire. A chest pops up in the room across the hall!
  3. Before you go back, turn around. A Gold Skulltula hides on the wall! Hit it with your Boomerang. That is Skulltula 1 of 5 (kid).
  4. Go back to the main room. Go in the left room. A crystal switch sits behind a grate on the other side. Throw your Boomerang to the left so it curves around and hits the crystal. A bridge drops down!
  5. Go in the next room. An Anubis waits here. It copies your moves! Go west and hit the eye on the pillar. Then stand on the pillar. The Anubis copies you and stands on the pillar across from you. Fire burns it up! It burns itself to death.
  6. The next room has 5 crystals to grab. Two are on wall grates you can climb. Grab all five. Open the chest for Small Key 1. Go through the bridge.
  7. Crawl through the gap in the middle of the left and right rooms. Use Small Key 1 on the door with the climbable wall.
  8. Two Lizalfos attack in the next room. Beat them. Hit the crystal switch with your Slingshot. A chest pops up with 5 Bombchus! You need them next.
  9. Look right. A cracked wall sits high up where normal bombs cannot reach. Throw a Bombchu at it! The wall blows open. The door in the next room opens. Go in.
  10. A big statue stands here. A Gold Skulltula sits on the statue's right side! Hit it with your Boomerang. That is Skulltula 2 of 5 (kid).
  11. Use a Deku Stick on the lit brazier. Light the unlit torch with it. A chest pops up with the Dungeon Map!
  12. Go to the southwest side, up the stairs. A button sits on the floor. Push the statue onto the button! A room opens.
  13. Inside, grab 5 more crystals to open the next door. But first, look at the cube with a sun on it. Pull it into the beam of light on the floor! A chest pops out of a hole in the wall. It holds Small Key 2.
  14. Turn around after you get the key. A Gold Skulltula sits above the door! Hit it with your Boomerang. That is Skulltula 3 of 5 (kid).
  15. Go in the next room. An Iron Knuckle (a knight in heavy armor) attacks! He is slow but strong. Dodge his big axe swing. Hit him after he swings. Back off fast. When his armor falls off, he gets fast! Be ready to run and roll.
  16. Beat the Iron Knuckle. Go down the path outside. A chest sits on your left. Open it. You get the Silver Gauntlets! They let you lift big gray rocks — but only as a grown-up. Your kid arms are too small!
  17. Warp back to the Temple of Time. Pull the Master Sword out. You are a grown-up again! Play the Requiem of Spirit to warp back to the desert.
KID TIP! As a grown-up, ride the Magic Bean plant you planted outside the temple door. It takes you up to a Gold Skulltula and Heart Piece #36! Shoot the Heart Piece off the arch with your Bow.

THE ADULT SIDE — GETTING THE COMPASS

  1. Go in as Adult Link. A big stone block stops the way. Use your Silver Gauntlets to push it out of the way! Hit the switch on the ceiling to open the doors.
  2. Go in the room on the left. Beat the Wolfos. Stand on the Triforce mark on the floor, facing north. Play Zelda's Lullaby. A chest pops up! Grab it with your Longshot. It holds the Compass!

THE ADULT SIDE — BOULDERS AND SMALL KEYS

  1. Go right from the Compass room. Rolling boulders roll down the hall! Grab the five crystals. They hide in the lower rooms on the left and right. A Gold Skulltula hides in the far-left room! Hit it with your Longshot. That is Skulltula 4 of 5 (grown-up).
  2. Play the Song of Time in front of the blue block to move it. It shows the last crystal. Grab it. A room opens. A Like Like waits inside! Stun it with your Longshot and slash it. Open the chest for Small Key 3.
  3. Go back to the room with the switch and the blade trap. Use Small Key 3 on the door. Climb up the wall to the next room.
  4. This room has mirrors and sun faces on the walls. Turn the light toward the sun on the east wall. A chest falls from the ceiling! Skip the next sun — it wakes a Wallmaster. Turn the light toward the sun on the south wall. The door opens! The last sun makes a fake chest. Skip it too.
  5. Go in the room with the big statue. Longshot to the statue's left wrist (on your right). You see a Triforce mark. Play Zelda's Lullaby. A chest pops up on the statue's right hand!
  6. Before you grab it, Longshot to the northeast corner of the room. Hit the pressure plate with your Megaton Hammer. A door opens.
  7. Drop down. Longshot to the statue's right wrist (on your left). Grab the chest. It holds Small Key 4.
  8. From this chest, play the Scarecrow's Song. Pierre pops up! Longshot to him. A Gold Skulltula sits on the floor up there. That is Skulltula 5 of 5 (grown-up).

GETTING THE MIRROR SHIELD

  1. Drop down. Go through the door in front of the statue. Push the big stone block so it falls down. Follow the path. Push the next stone block down too. This makes a path back to the start.
  2. Go back to the statue room. Longshot up above the door in the southeast corner. Go upstairs. Use Small Key 4 on the door.
  3. Three Anubis and a Beamos wait in the next room! Play the Song of Time in front of the wall mark. A time block shows up. Step on the button in the northeast to light the fire pit.
  4. Now trick each Anubis onto the fire. Jump over the time block. Go to the northwest and southwest corners. Each Anubis copies you and walks into the fire! When all three burn up, the next door opens.
  5. The next room has a button on the floor. An Armos (a stone statue that comes alive) walks around. Trick it into standing on the button! The south door opens.
  6. Go through the door. Fight another Iron Knuckle! Dodge his axe. Hit him after he swings. When his armor falls off, he gets fast. Beat him!
  7. Go in the next room. Follow the path outside. Step on the button on the floor. A chest pops up with the Mirror Shield! It can bounce light and magic.

GETTING THE BOSS KEY

  1. Go back to the north room on floor 3 where the sunlight shines in. Now you have the Mirror Shield! Stand in the light. Hold up your shield. Bounce the light onto the sun on the wall. The door opens! Go in. A chest holds Small Key 5.
  2. Take Small Key 5 to the locked door by the room where you fought the Anubis. Climb the wall. Use your Longshot to help you reach the top.
  3. In the next room, play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce mark to open the door. Beat the Torch Slugs inside with your Megaton Hammer.
  4. Break the door in the northwest corner with your Hammer. Find the glowing eye on the wall. Hit it! The wall falls down. A platform shows up. Longshot to the ceiling to reach it.
  5. Step on the button to turn off the fire around the chest. Longshot back over. Open the chest. You get the Boss Key!
  6. Go back to the last room and climb the wall. Go through the door. Drop down and hit the switch to open the door above.
  7. Bomb the rock wall. Use the light to turn the mirror to the last room. Aim the light at the other light device. Go in the next room. Aim the light at the bars. They open!
  8. Follow the light and drop down. Stand on the platform with the sun mark. Use your Mirror Shield to bounce the light onto the sun on the wall. A platform drops down in front of the statue's head. Bounce the light onto the statue's head! Its face falls off. The boss door shows up behind it!
KID TIP! The Iron Knuckle is slow but strong. Hit him, then back off fast. When his armor falls off, he gets fast — be ready to run and roll! And watch out for Wallmasters in the sun rooms. If a hand grabs you, it drags you back to the temple door.
BOSS: TWINROVA
Two twin witches — one shoots fire, one shoots ice! First, a third Iron Knuckle jumps out at you. Beat him first. Then the twins fly in. Hold up your Mirror Shield! When one twin shoots magic at you, the shield catches it. It bounces back at the OTHER twin. Fire hurts the ice twin. Ice hurts the fire twin. Keep bouncing their magic back at each other! After enough hits, they join into one big witch: Twinrova! Now she shoots fire and ice at you. Hold up your Mirror Shield and catch her magic. You must catch the SAME kind three times in a row! If you catch fire, fire, then ice, it does not count. Catch fire three times in a row (or ice three times in a row). Then she falls down! Run in and slash her with your sword as much as you can. Then do it all again. When she falls for the last time, slash her until she is gone!
YOU GET: Silver Gauntlets, Mirror Shield, Heart Container, Spirit Medallion, Requiem of Spirit
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas (5): Kid side (3): (1) On the wall in the right room after the Din's Fire braziers. (2) On the big statue's right side, past the Bombchu wall. (3) Above the door after the sun-cube Small Key. Grown-up side (2): (4) Far-left room in the rolling-boulder hall. (5) With Pierre the scarecrow in the big statue room (play the Scarecrow's Song).
• Small Keys (5): Kid: (1) Five-crystal room past the Anubis. (2) Sun-cube room. Grown-up: (3) Like Like room past the Song of Time block. (4) Chest on the statue's hand. (5) Sun room with the Mirror Shield.
• Boss Key: In the chest past the eye switch and the fire ring, on the grown-up side.
• Plant a Magic Bean at the Desert Colossus as a kid for Heart Piece #36 (shoot it off the arch with your Bow as a grown-up).
• Heart Pieces #34 and #35 are in Gerudo's Fortress (Scarecrow chest + Horseback Archery) — do them as a grown-up before the Spirit Temple.
• Skulltulas: 4 in Gerudo Valley, 2 in the Fortress, 3 at the Desert Colossus.
