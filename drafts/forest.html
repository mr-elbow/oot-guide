8. FOREST TEMPLE

An old temple in the woods where the halls twist and turn.

Goal: Wake Saria, the Sage of Forest. The temple is twisty — the halls turn and change!

Before you go in, get the Hookshot first. See the bonus chapter just before this one! You need it to climb and cross gaps in here.

GETTING IN

  1. Go back to Kokiri Forest. It is full of monsters now! Sheik meets you outside and teaches you the Minuet of Forest.
  2. Climb up to the temple door. Go in. Two Wolfos (big gray wolves) jump out at you! Watch both of them. Dodge back when one swipes. Then jump in and hit it when its back is turned.
  3. Beat both Wolfos. The front door is locked. Look up at the big tree on the east side (your right). See the long wall of vines? Climb up the vines.
  4. Run along the high branch to the tree on the other side. Open the small chest up there. It holds Small Key 1!
  5. Use Small Key 1 to open the front door. A big Skulltula blocks the hall. Hit its back side with your sword.
KID TIP! Skulltula 1 of 5: While you are up on the high trees, look back at the top of the vine wall on the east tree. A Gold Skulltula hides up there. Hit it with your Hookshot!

GETTING THE DUNGEON MAP

  1. You are in the big main room, called the foyer. It has a lift in the middle and four colored torches. Four ghost sisters — the Poe Sisters — steal the four flames! The lift stops working. You must win back all four flames.
  2. First, walk straight ahead toward the green window. Go up the stairs. Turn right. A Gold Skulltula hides on the wall by a blue window. Hit it with your Hookshot. That is Skulltula 2 of 5.
  3. Go north through the door under the green window. A Blue Bubble (a flying skull with blue fire) will ram you. Hold up your shield! The fire goes out when it hits your shield. Then slash it fast.
  4. Go in the next room. The door locks behind you! Two Stalfos (skeleton knights) attack, one at a time. Be patient. Wait for one to swing twice. Hit it after the second swing. Then dodge the big overhand hit! If one jumps far, jump to the side. Beat both of them.
  5. A small chest pops up. Open it for Small Key 2. Go back to the foyer.
  6. Do NOT use the key on the red door yet. Look to the northwest. A big blue block stops a door on the lower floor. Play the Song of Time in front of it. The block goes away! Go through to the northwest courtyard outside.
  7. This yard has a pond and a big well in the middle. Deku Babas hide in the grass. On the right, vines climb up to a high balcony. Use your Hookshot to kill the Skullwalltulas (spiders) on the vines first. Then climb up.
  8. The way ahead is broken. Hookshot across to the balcony to the north. Step on the switch there. The water in the well goes down!
  9. Jump down into the well. Swim to the path in the northeast. Follow the low hall. Open the small chest for Small Key 3.
  10. Climb the ladder up into the other courtyard. Octoroks spit rocks at you from a small moat. Block their rocks with your shield. Then Hookshot across the water to a big chest. Open it. It holds the Dungeon Map!
  11. Go back through the door to the foyer. Step on the switch to open the gate on the blue side of the room.

GETTING THE FAIRY BOW

  1. Use a key on the east red door. Beat the Blue Bubble in the hall. Go in the big tall room.
  2. Watch the floor! A dark shadow grows under you. The light above turns from blue to red. DASH away fast! A giant hand called a Wallmaster slams down. If it grabs you, it drags you all the way back to the temple door!
  3. After it slams down and misses, run in and slash it before it floats away. One Wallmaster down.
  4. Climb the ladder. You see yellow arrows on the floor. They point south from a big stone block to the west. Go around the balcony to the other side of the block. Push it east, then south. Go back around and push it west until it drops into a hole in the floor.
  5. Now climb up to the second floor. Push the next block south until it stops. Go back down and up the other ledge. Push the block east until it drops into a hole. Now you can climb up to floor 3.
  6. Beat the little Blue Bubbles with your Hookshot. Look west for a switch. Step on it. It turns on a lift so you can ride up and down this tall room fast.
  7. Go through the north door. You are in a strange twisted hall! The whole room tilts to the left. Hop along the tilted floors. Watch out for another Wallmaster. Cross to the door on the east side.
  8. Go down the long stairs. You see paintings on the walls. One painting looks like a Poe Sister! It runs away when you get close. Ignore it for now. Go down to floor 2 and go through the far door.
  9. Three Stalfos attack here, one after the other. The middle of the room is a pit of green poison goo! Stay near the walls. Let them come to you. Do not dodge into the goo.
  10. Beat all three Stalfos. A big chest pops up. Open it. You get the Fairy Bow! Now you can shoot arrows far away.
KID TIP! Stalfos trick: when a Stalfos jumps high across the room, hit it mid-jump with your Hookshot! It falls down. Then hit it with a spin attack.

THE POE SISTERS — JOELLE AND BETH

There are four Poe Sisters: Meg (purple, the oldest), Joelle (red), Beth (blue), and Amy (green, the youngest). Each one hides in paintings. Shoot her painting with your Bow to make her come out!

  1. Go back to the stair room. Shoot the Poe paintings with your Bow. Shoot one on each side of the floor 2 landing. Shoot one more at the top of the floor 3 stairs. With all three paintings gone, the Poe must come out and fight!
  2. Joelle (red) spins at you with her torch. Dodge to the side when she spins. She gets dizzy after her spin. Run in and slash her! Hit her again after her next spin. When she falls, her red flame flies back to the foyer. A small chest pops up with Small Key 4.
  3. Now go to the blue side of the temple (the west wing). It looks just like the red side. Shoot the Poe paintings on the floor 2 and floor 3 landings.
  4. Beth (blue) fights just like Joelle. Dodge her spin. Hit her when she is dizzy. When she falls, her blue flame flies back. A big chest pops up with the Compass!

GETTING THE BOSS KEY

  1. Look at your map. There is a locked door on floor 3 past where you beat Beth. You need one more key. Go back east, down through the red wing and the twisted red hall, and outside.
  2. Remember the eye switch in the twisted hall? Shoot the eye with your Bow. The hall straightens out! The red chapel room turns back to normal too.
  3. Before you go in, drop to floor 2 in the tall block room. Walk along the yellow arrows to the small balcony in the northeast corner. Shoot the eye switch there with your Bow. A small chest drops down. It holds a Purple Rupee (50 rupees)!
  4. Go back through the straight red hall. The red chapel room now has a hole in the floor. Next to the hole is a big blue chest. Open it. It holds the Boss Key! This is the key to the boss door.

THE POE SISTERS — AMY AND MEG

  1. Drop down the hole by the Boss Key chest. You land in a pool on floor 1. Get out fast and beat the Blue Bubbles.
  2. Go out to the south. You are on a low balcony over the northwest courtyard. Do NOT jump down yet. Look for a door to the south. A small ledge wraps around past it. Follow it. A Gold Skulltula hides on the wall. Hit it with your Hookshot. That is Skulltula 3 of 5.
  3. Go in the room to the west. A Floormaster (a giant green hand) attacks! It has green fire. Hold up your shield to put the fire out. Or dodge its hit and slash it after the flames go down.
  4. When a Floormaster dies, it splits into three small hands! Slash them all fast. If one grabs you, it can grow back into a big hand. Din's Fire works great here. When they are all gone, a small chest pops up with Small Key 5.
  5. Go through the south door. This is a shortcut back up to floor 3. Shoot the eye to twist the red hall again. Now go back through to the blue wing. Use Small Key 5 on the locked door on the top floor of the stairwell.
  6. Go through the blue chapel and up a ladder. Shoot the Green Bubbles from far away with your Bow. Open the far door.
  7. You are in a cold room. The eye switch above the door is frozen in ice! Step on the switch nearby to light a torch. Shoot an arrow through the torch flame. The fire arrow melts the ice off the eye. Shoot the eye to twist the blue hall!
  8. The blue chapel twists too. A pit opens down to floor 1. Fall down into the northeast corner of floor 1.
  9. Find the side door out to the courtyard. Push the stone block to make a shortcut. Turn around and look up. See the straw on the wall? If you learned the Scarecrow's Song at Lake Hylia, play it here. Pierre the scarecrow pops up! Hookshot to him. A Gold Skulltula hides up there with him. That is Skulltula 4 of 5.
  10. Go back inside. Watch the ceiling! Big tiles fall down in a checkerboard pattern. Move only when the trap goes up. Stay in the rays of light — the trap does not fall there. Watch out for Skulltulas that drop down in the light!
  11. Step on the switch under the light to open the far door. Grab the small chest with 5 Arrows on the way.
  12. The next room has a big block in the middle with a painting on it. A painting on the wall shows the green Poe. Shoot her with your Bow. She jumps into the block painting and breaks it into pieces!
  13. Slide the blocks to put her picture back together. Trick: she adds one fake block. The fake piece has a dark tassel on top of the hood. Do not use it. Use the light-colored piece as the top right corner. When the picture is whole, Amy (green) comes out to fight!
  14. Amy spins at you many times in a row. Dodge each spin. Wait until she gets dizzy. Then slash her! When she falls, her green flame flies back. The door to the foyer opens.
  15. Go down the hall. A big painting of the last Poe Sister blocks your path. Stay near the door. Shoot her when she comes into view. She runs back into the foyer. Chase her!
  16. Meg (purple) makes three fake copies of herself! The fakes cannot be hurt. First time: shoot the one holding the green flame torch. Second time: shoot the one with a lit torch. Third time: all of them have purple torches! Look at their eyes. The fakes have narrow eyes. The real Meg has wide eyes. Shoot the real one!
  17. Hit Meg three times with your Bow, then slash her with your sword each time. When she falls, all four torches light up! The lift in the foyer starts to work. Ride it down to the basement.

UNLOCKING THE LIFT TO THE BOSS

  1. You are in a round basement room. The walls have handholds. You must push the walls around to open new doors. First, look for the open door to the south. Beat the Skulltula inside. A Gold Skulltula hides in the wall of the alcove. Hit it with your Hookshot. That is Skulltula 5 of 5!
  2. See the gate with a hole? Behind it is a frozen eye switch. You need fire to melt the ice. Push the handholds counter-clockwise (to the left).
  3. Keep pushing until the open door moves to the northwest. Step on the switch inside. A torch lights up in one of the small windows!
  4. Keep pushing counter-clockwise. Stop at the southwest alcove to open a chest with 5 Arrows. Keep pushing until the lit torch window is in front of the room with the frozen eye.
  5. Shoot an arrow through the torch flame. The fire arrow melts the ice. Shoot the eye switch. The gate to the north opens! Use your Boss Key on the boss door.
BOSS: PHANTOM GANON
He rides a horse out of paintings on the walls! There are six paintings. Watch them all. The fakes turn back at the edge of the painting. Only the real one jumps out. Trick: the real Phantom Ganon holds his spear in his right hand. The fakes hold it in their left hand. Shoot the one with the spear on the left side of the painting! Shoot him with your Bow 2 or 3 times. When he falls off his horse, slash him with your sword. Then he floats up high and throws green magic balls at you. Hit a ball back at him with your sword, like tennis! When he falls down, run up and slash him a lot. He may dive at you instead of throwing a ball — dodge fast! Hit him 3 times and he is done!
YOU GET: Fairy Bow, Heart Container, Forest Medallion, Minuet of Forest
KID TIP! The Poe sisters are Meg, Joelle, Beth, and Amy. Meg is the oldest. Amy is the youngest. If you lose track, check your Threads of Time!
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas (5): (1) Top of the vine wall on the east tree at the entrance. (2) Foyer, on the wall by a blue window near the green-window stairs. (3) On the ledge past the south door on the balcony over the northwest courtyard. (4) With Pierre the scarecrow in the northeast courtyard (play the Scarecrow's Song). (5) In the south alcove of the basement room, past the lift.
• Small Keys (5): Tree branches at the entrance, Stalfos room, well basement, Joelle's chest, Floormaster room.
• Boss Key: Big blue chest in the red chapel room after you straighten the twisted hall.
