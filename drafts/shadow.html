11. SHADOW TEMPLE

Spooky! Full of fake walls and hidden rooms.

Goal: Wake Impa, the Sage of Shadow. This temple is spooky and full of fake walls!

Before you go in, you need the Lens of Truth from the Bottom of the Well chapter just before this one! The Lens shows you things that are not really there. Put it on a lot in this temple.

GETTING IN

  1. Go to the graveyard in Kakariko. Play Zelda's Lullaby at the big tomb to open the Shadow Temple. Sheik teaches you the Nocturne of Shadow before you go in.
  2. The first room has a sign: "Those who lack the eyes of truth are not granted the way forward." Put on your Lens of Truth! You see a ring of unlit torches that were not there before.
  3. Stand in the middle of the room. Use Din's Fire. All the torches light up at once! The sealed door opens.
  4. Go through the hall. Flying Pots zoom at you! Hold up your shield. They smash into it and break. Turn the corner. A big gap blocks the way. Use your Longshot to cross to the far side.
  5. A sign on the wall says: "Without the eyes of truth, there is no path." Put on your Lens of Truth. The wall is fake! Walk right through the creepy face into the room past it.
  6. More creepy face walls are here. A big bird statue stands in the middle. It has a handhold. A sign says: "The tip of my beak seeks the skull of truth. Should it find falsehood, darkness shall fall."
  7. Look at the small columns with skulls around the statue. Put on your Lens of Truth. All but one skull column goes away! Only one is real — the skull of truth. Push the bird statue around until its beak points to the northwest, at the real skull. The door to the east opens!
  8. At the end of the room is a big pit. The door is too far to reach. A sign says: "The valley of dead can only be crossed with footsteps as light as a feather." You need new boots to cross. You do not have them yet. Turn back for now.

GETTING THE HOVER BOOTS AND THE DUNGEON MAP

  1. Go back to where the skull of truth was. A hidden doorway hides behind it on the northwest wall. Your Lens shows it. Go through!
  2. You are in a room full of skulls. A black skull on the north wall hides another secret door. Put on your Lens. Go through the hidden door.
  3. The door locks behind you! A ReDead (a scary zombie) and some Keese attack. Play the Sun's Song to stun the ReDead! Or run behind it and slash its back so it cannot hit you with its scary scream. Beat them all. A big chest pops up with the Dungeon Map!
  4. The map shows hidden rooms you would miss without the Lens. Go west to a room just like the first skull room. A hidden opening hides on the west wall. Go through it.
  5. More Flying Pots attack in the next small room. A Wallmaster may drop from above too! Watch the floor. When the shadow grows and the light turns red, dash away. Then slash the hand before it floats off.
  6. Find the hidden exit in the northwest corner, heading west. Go down a long hall. The floor drops out from under you! Climb up the other side on the mesh wall.
  7. In the next room, an old foe waits: Dead Hand is back! He hides under the floor. His hands grab you. Let one hand grab you. Dead Hand pops up and walks toward you. Break free! Then slash him before he runs away.
  8. Do not run at him too fast. Other hands can grab you and leave you open. Your grown-up sword hurts him a lot. It will not take long. When he falls, a big chest pops up. Open it. You get the Hover Boots!
  9. Hover Boots let you float in the air for a short time when you walk off a ledge. They are the opposite of the Iron Boots! Press down on the D-pad to swap them on and off. They make you slide around like you are on ice. Take them off when you fight or walk normally.
  10. Try them out! Run back through the hall with the falling floor. The Hover Boots carry you right across!

GETTING THE COMPASS

  1. Go back to the bird statue room. Make sure the beak points northwest at the real skull. Put on your Hover Boots. Run across the pit to the creepy tongue platform on the far side!
  2. The next room has a Beamos (a statue that shoots lasers). Throw a bomb at it! A big cracked wall sits on the far side. Bomb that too!
  3. A locked door waits past it. Your map shows rooms to the north and south. Put on your Lens of Truth. Both walls are fake! Go south first.
  4. The door locks behind you! Two Gibdos (tough mummies) attack. They are like ReDeads but stronger. Use Din's Fire to burn both at once! Keep your distance so they cannot grab you and steal your life. Beat both of them. A big chest pops up with the Compass!
  5. Now go north. A big room has two tall statues that spin huge scythes around the room. Five Silver Rupees sit around the room. You must grab all five!
  6. Follow a scythe as it passes. Grab the first Silver Rupee. Race to the northeast side. Jump up on the thin wooden wall to grab the second one while the scythes pass below you.
  7. Jump to the alcove in the north for the third Silver Rupee. A sign here says: "Approach if you seek the ferry to the afterlife." Step forward. You fall down a hole you cannot see to floor B4! You see a big skeleton ship, but you cannot go on from here. Climb back up.
  8. Dash between the scythes to another wooden wall. Look for the fourth Silver Rupee on a high ledge. Longshot to the target up there to grab it.
  9. Time your jump down to the west alcove for the last Silver Rupee. All five are yours! A room opens on the other side with Small Key 1 inside. Grab it, but watch the scythes on your way over.
  10. Use Small Key 1 on the locked door back in the room where the Beamos was. Go down a long twisty hall. Look up! Drool drips from above. Skulltulas wait up there to drop on you. Hit them before they drop!
  11. Near the end of the hall, drop down. Guillotines (big falling blades) fall from above. Dodge them and the Skulltulas. At the end, ride the lift down to floor B3.

EXPLORING FLOOR B3

  1. You are in a big cave room with platforms over a deep pit. Time your jumps past the falling guillotines. Follow the road south to the first big platform.
  2. A Stalfos jumps out at you! There is not much room. Do not dodge into the pit! Let him swing first. Dodge around his wide swings. Then slash him.
  3. After he falls, you have two ways to go. East looks like a gap too big even for Hover Boots. Put on your Lens of Truth! An invisible platform floats halfway across. Put on your Hover Boots and float across it to the door.
  4. Before you go in the door, look in the small alcove to the north. A Business Scrub hides there! He sells magic and supplies if you are low. The Lens uses a lot of magic, so stock up if you need to.
  5. Go in the east room. It looks empty. Put on your Lens! A giant invisible spinning scythe trap spins in the middle. The far door is locked until you beat all the bad guys: some Fire Keese and a Like Like on the far side.
  6. Shoot the Keese with arrows first. Then dash through as the scythe passes. Run fast past the Like Like! If you stop, it will eat you and steal your shield. If it eats you, stun it with your Longshot and slash it to get your shield back.
  7. When all the bad guys are gone, the east door opens. Inside are two small chests with a Red Rupee in each. One chest is invisible — use your Lens to find it! A Gold Skulltula hides on the far wall. That is Skulltula 1 of 5.
  8. Go back to the big cave room. Take the west path to three big platforms that go up and down. Wait for each one to reach the top before you step on the next one.
  9. On the third platform, look down. A Beamos sits below with two spinning spike traps. Five more Silver Rupees are down there! Grab the first one from the platform. You can float into it with your Hover Boots when the platform is high up.
  10. Land below. Throw a bomb at the Beamos. Climb the scaffolding to the south. Use your Hover Boots to cross the gap for the second Silver Rupee. Jump back down as a spike trap passes to grab the third one.
  11. Walk through the spinning spikes with care. Grab the fourth Silver Rupee just past the sealed door. Walk to the north edge. Two rat statues stand there with chains between them. The last Silver Rupee floats in the air — but it is really on an invisible platform! Put on your Lens to see it. Grab it. The door opens!
  12. Go through the door to the west. Two big spike traps rise up and fall down. A sign says: "Only one with eyes of truth can find the stone umbrella that shields against a rain of blades." Put on your Lens! A hidden hole sits to the right of the traps. Inside is a big stone block.
  13. Pull the block all the way out until it drops into a small hole. Then push it from behind. Push it a little at a time under the falling spikes. The block shields you like an umbrella! Keep pushing until you and the block are past the second spike trap. Climb up on the block to the ledge above.
  14. Look on the south side. A small hole in the wall hides a small chest with a Red Rupee. On the north side, a slow spike trap moves back and forth. Behind the bars there is a Fairy's Thought. It wants a gust of wind. Play the Song of Storms! Fairy magic heals you.
  15. Now ride the top of a falling spike trap to cross to the other side of the ledge. A second small chest holds Small Key 2!
  16. Look up at the northeast corner of the room. A Gold Skulltula sits on a high ledge. Ride the falling spike traps up like lifts to reach it! That is Skulltula 2 of 5.
  17. Go back to the room where you got the Silver Rupees. Go north to the two rat statues. Put on your Lens. A wooden platform below moves back and forth between the statues. Time your drop! Land on it. Go through the door with Small Key 2.
  18. The next room has more Silver Rupees and two ReDeads in the corners. Put on your Lens! Invisible spike traps cover the floor. Keep the Lens on while you fight. Stun the ReDeads with the Sun's Song so you do not dodge into a spike!
  19. Beat the ReDeads. Grab the first Silver Rupee in the middle. The second one floats high in the southwest corner. Longshot to the hidden target above it. Do the same for the third one in the northeast. The last one is in the northwest corner on a high invisible ledge. Longshot up, then jump off the side to grab it. The west door opens!
  20. The next room has Fire Keese flying over a big black skull cauldron. A sign says: "A dark orb must be cast into the black skull." You have a dark orb: a bomb! Beat the Keese first. Climb to the top floor. More Keese hide in the walls. Throw a bomb into the cauldron. It blows up and shows a new Small Key 3!
  21. Go back to the last room. Longshot up to the northwest platform. Use your Hover Boots to run across to the other ledge on the north. Open the locked door with Small Key 3.
  22. The next hall has big fans that blow you backward! Put on your Iron Boots to stand your ground. Walk slow past the spike traps. Go up.
  23. After the second fan, a third fan sits across a big gap. Wait for a break between gusts. Run across fast with your Hover Boots! Then swap back to your Iron Boots fast so the wind does not blow you back. Look back the way you came. A Gold Skulltula hides just below the ledge! That is Skulltula 3 of 5.
  24. Drop into the lower hall. Pits sit on both sides. More fans blow you around. Keep your Iron Boots on. Check the alcoves on the left and right. Use your Longshot to break the pots and grab what is inside.
  25. Go through the far door. Two more ReDeads wait. Stun them both at once with the Sun's Song. Then hit them with a full spin attack! A sign behind them says: "Lighten your steps and seek the hidden path of the wind." Put on your Lens. A small chest hides in the corner. It holds a Business Scrub Seed! Go back to the last hall.
  26. Two of the fans blow into alcoves with pots. One fan blows at nothing at all — until you put on your Lens! A hidden path floats there. Put on your Hover Boots. Wait for the fan to blow. Run with the wind! It boosts you all the way across the gap.
  27. The next room has two Gibdos between moving spike traps. Stun them with the Sun's Song to make it easier. Beat them both. A small chest pops up to the west. It holds only one Green Rupee! Look at the east wall instead. It is cracked. Bomb it! Behind it, your Lens shows an invisible chest with Small Key 4!
  28. Open the north door with Small Key 4. Go down into the deep part of the temple.

FINDING THE BOSS KEY IN B4

  1. Ride the lift down. You come out on a creepy dock. A big skeleton ship waits here. It is the same ship you saw when you fell down the hole in the scythe room! Pull the stone block to the left. It makes a shortcut back up to floor B2.
  2. Pull the block out until it reaches the far wooden platforms. Climb up onto the docks. Look back west, above the lift. A high ledge has straw on it! Play the Scarecrow's Song. Pierre pops up. Hookshot over to him. A Gold Skulltula hides up there! That is Skulltula 4 of 5.
  3. Step onto the creepy ship. Look for the Triforce mark on the deck. Play Zelda's Lullaby. The ship comes to life and starts to sail!
  4. A Stalfos jumps on the ship! There is not much room. Bait out his big swings. Dodge and slash him. A second Stalfos jumps on right after. Beat him fast!
  5. The ship reaches the far shore. It has no brakes! It slams into the wall and starts to sink. Jump off fast!
  6. On land, you see two giant bird statues. One has fallen over. The other one stands on a cracked base. Blow up the base with a bomb! The statue falls over and makes a bridge to the far side. But the way south leads to the boss room, and you still need one more key. Go west first.
  7. Burn the webs around the door with Din's Fire or a Fire Arrow. Go in. The room looks strange. Floormasters walk around, but they do not see you. When you run at one, you hit a wall you cannot see! Put on your Lens of Truth. The room is a maze of invisible walls!
  8. Keep your Lens on. Use it to see when you have a clear path to a Floormaster. Beat them all. Remember: when a Floormaster dies, three small hands pop out! Use Din's Fire or a fast spin attack to kill them all at once.
  9. There are three doors in the maze room. The north one is locked. Go south first. Keep your Lens on! An invisible Floormaster hides in the next room. Beat it and its little hands. A chest pops up with Small Key 5 — the last one!
  10. Before you use it, go through the west door. This room has three big black skull cauldrons. Throw a bomb into each one! One blows up and shows a pile of rupees — and the last Gold Skulltula in this temple! That is Skulltula 5 of 5.
  11. Now open the north door with Small Key 5. It is a trap! Big spiked walls close in from both sides! Do not panic. Look at the walls. They are covered in cobwebs. Cobwebs burn! Use Din's Fire to burn the walls away.
  12. Behind the walls sit two ReDeads. Each one guards a chest. One chest holds a big magic jar. The other chest is the big blue one. Open it. You get the Boss Key!
  13. Go back the way you came to the docks. Cross the bird statue you knocked down. Run up onto the other fallen statue. Look up above the west wall over the pit. A high ledge has straw on it! Play the Scarecrow's Song. Hookshot up to Pierre. Pots up there hold items. One pot holds a Fairy!
  14. When you are ready, go south. A giant pit sits between you and the boss door. Put on your Lens of Truth to see the invisible platforms. Put on your Hover Boots. Float across to the far side. Go down into the lair of the dark beast.
BOSS: BONGO BONGO
He is a giant ghost with big hands and one big eye — and you can only see his body with the Lens of Truth! The room is a big drum. Each time he beats the drum, you bounce high in the air. Put on your Hover Boots! They keep you floating in place so you do not bounce. His hands are the first problem. Watch the shadows above you. When a hand slams down, roll away fast! After it slams, it stops glowing. Shoot it with your Bow or hit it with your Hookshot. Do both hands. Then his body shows up! Put on your Lens of Truth. His neck opens to show a big glowing eye. He slams his body down and charges at you. Shoot his eye with your Bow to stun him! Take off your Hover Boots and run in. Slash him a lot with your sword while he is down. Then put your Hover Boots back on for the next round. Later he sweeps a hand across the room like a backhand. It can knock you off the platform! If you fall in the poison goo, climb back fast on the small platforms. He may also slam both hands together to squash you. Strafe to the side while you shoot. In the last part, he grabs low at you or punches fast with one fist. Run sideways away from the punch! Then shoot the hand when it slams down. Stun his eye one last time. Run in and hit him with everything you have!
YOU GET: Hover Boots, Heart Container, Shadow Medallion, Nocturne of Shadow
KID TIP! The Lens of Truth uses magic. If your magic runs out, look for green magic jars. And always check fake walls with the Lens — there are secret rooms with treasure! The Lens comes from the Bottom of the Well, NOT from this temple. If you do not have it yet, go back and do that chapter first.
WHILE YOU'RE HERE! ⭐
• Skulltulas (5): (1) Far wall of the room past the invisible scythe trap, east of floor B3. (2) High ledge in the falling-spike-trap room — ride the traps up like lifts. (3) Just below the ledge in the fan hall, after the third fan. (4) High ledge at the skeleton ship docks, with Pierre the scarecrow (play the Scarecrow's Song). (5) In the three-cauldron room — bomb all three cauldrons.
• Small Keys (5): (1) Scythe room, after all 5 Silver Rupees. (2) Chest on the ledge in the falling-spike-trap room. (3) Bomb the black skull cauldron. (4) Invisible chest behind the cracked wall in the Gibdo room. (5) Floormaster maze room, south door.
• Boss Key: Big blue chest in the spike-wall trap room — burn the cobweb walls with Din's Fire.
• Grown-up Heart Pieces: #9 (windmill, through Dampé's tunnel) and #14 (Dampé's race — Hookshot needed) in Kakariko, plus #13 (ride the graveyard bean plant you planted as a kid).
