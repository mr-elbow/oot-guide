4. KAKARIKO VILLAGE

A quiet village with a windmill, a graveyard, and lots of secrets.

Goal: Meet the people, get new gear, and get ready for Death Mountain.

FIRST STOP: LON LON RANCH

On your way to Kakariko, stop at Lon Lon Ranch. It is right in the middle of Hyrule Field. You must do this as a kid!

  1. Walk into the big horse field. Malon stands in the middle, singing to a small horse. The horse runs away when you get close. Talk to Malon.
  2. Malon asks if you have an instrument. Take out your Fairy Ocarina with D-pad Left. Play her tune back when the notes show up. You learn Epona's Song! The little horse, Epona, runs right to you.
  3. Talk to Malon again. She tells you to visit her dad inside the main house. Go in. Talon is asleep in a pile of Cuccos! Talk to him to wake him up.
  4. Talon wants to play a game. Find his 3 Super Cuccos hiding among the normal Cuccos. You have 30 seconds! Trick: throw all the normal Cuccos behind Talon's desk first. Then only the Super Cuccos are left out in the open. Super Cuccos stand stiff and tall, with their wings down and their heads held high.
  5. Win and you get a bottle of Lon Lon Milk — and you keep the bottle! That is Bottle #3. Drink the milk (2 sips) to keep the empty bottle.
  6. Go to the old stone tower in the south-west corner of the ranch. Inside are 9 milk crates. Pull the closest crates out of the middle row. Then pull back the crate in the middle against the wall. A small gap opens. Crawl through it to find Heart Piece #3!
  7. Behind the main house, on the north-east side, roll into the tall thin tree by the wooden tower. A Gold Skulltula falls out!

KAKARIKO VILLAGE

  1. Go east from Hyrule Field to Kakariko Village. Walk up the stone stairs. The guard at the door points out the big spots in town. Talk to everyone!
  2. First, the Cucco roundup! Find Anju, the Cucco Lady, by the big house that used to be Impa's. All 7 of her Cuccos ran away. You must catch them all and toss them back into her pen. Here is where they hide:
  3. Cucco 1: In the building site near Anju. It wanders around the scaffolding in the middle.
  4. Cucco 2: By the village gate where you came in.
  5. Cucco 3: In a wooden crate near the middle of town, by the house where the lady cooks food. Smash the crate!
  6. Cucco 4: On top of a pile of bricks. Carry Cucco 3 up to Impa's house. Glide down with it onto the bricks. Grab Cucco 4!
  7. Cucco 5: On the path up to Death Mountain Trail. Go north, up the stairs, past the tall wooden tower.
  8. Cuccos 6 and 7: Carry a Cucco up to the windmill on the west side of town. Glide over the gap in the fence. Throw your Cucco back over the fence so it does not get stuck! Cucco 6 is below the closed Potion House. Cucco 7 is on the hilltop behind the windmill. Climb the ladder to reach it.
  9. While you are on that hilltop, jump onto the roof of Granny's Potion Shop. Walk to the far side, near the wooden tower. A man lounges on the lower blue roof. He is so happy to see you! He gives you Heart Piece #7.
  10. When all 7 Cuccos are back in the pen, Anju gives you an Empty Bottle. That is Bottle #2!
  11. Climb the tall wooden tower in the middle of town. Talk to the man at the top. He tells you about the well and the windmill. The view is great too!
  12. Go to the windmill on the west side. Inside, the windmill man hums a strange tune. He is looking for the right song. You do not know it yet. Remember him! You will come back as a grown-up.
  13. Visit the House of Skulltula on the north side of town, below the windmill. It is covered in cobwebs. Inside lives a cursed family, half spider! Bring them Gold Skulltula tokens. Every 10 tokens earns a prize. 10 tokens = Adult's Wallet (holds 500 rupees)! 20 tokens = Echo Gem. It glows near spiders you missed!
  14. At night, look for Skulltulas in town. One hides in a web just left of the House of Skulltula door. One hides high on the red brick wall of the building under construction — climb the scaffolding. One hides on the ladder of the tall wooden tower. One hides on the back wall of the little boy's house, south of the tower. Roll into the narrow tree by the big central tree too!
  15. Go to the shops. The Bazaar sells a Hylian Shield. It is strong and fire can not burn it. Do not buy it yet! You can get one free in the graveyard. The Potion Shop is closed for now. Granny is away.
  16. Now the graveyard, behind the village. By day, a little boy wanders the graves. He pretends to be Dampé the gravedigger. If you pull a grave stone, he says he will tattle. Come back at night!
  17. At night, Dampé the gravedigger walks the grounds. Talk to him to play the Heart-Pounding Grave-Digging Tour. It costs 10 rupees per dig. He digs where you point. Sometimes you get rupees. Sometimes nothing. Keep digging until he says something heavy is down there. Pay 5 more rupees. He digs up Heart Piece #11!
  18. Pull the grave stones from the back. Some are empty. Some hide a green rupee. Some hide a Poe ghost! Poes turn invisible when you target them with ZL. Run away until they show up again. Then run in fast and slash them. You can catch a Poe in a bottle, but do not drink it. It hurts you!
  19. At the back of the graveyard stand 3 big monuments. The middle one has the royal crest. The two harp-shaped ones hide the Composer Brothers, Sharp and Flat. Talk to them and they attack like Poes! Beat them both. They tell you their sad tale. They hid a song in the Royal Family's Tomb.
  20. Stand on the royal crest on the floor and play Zelda's Lullaby. The tomb opens! Go in.
  21. Room 1: bats called Keese hide in the walls. They dive at you. Shoot them with your Slingshot or block with your shield, then slash.
  22. Room 2: green bubbling goo hurts you if you step in it. Redeads stalk the room. If one screams, you freeze! Then it grabs you and drains your hearts. Mash the buttons to break free. Sneak along the left side, then dart down the middle. Run out the far door fast!
  23. Room 3: read the poem on the stone plaque. Then play the song on the floor. You learn the Sun's Song! It turns night to day and day to night. It also puts Redeads to sleep!
  24. Look at the walls on your way out. There is a blue mark — Fairy's Writing. Play the Sun's Song there. A fairy heals you all the way!
  25. Go back to Room 2. Play the Sun's Song near the Redeads. They fall asleep! Now you can beat them safely.
  26. More grave secrets! From the graveyard door, go up the first small steps. Find the grave with flowers and butterflies on it, second from the top in the first row. Pull it back. There is a tomb below, but the door inside is sealed. You need bombs to open it. Come back later!
  27. Go up to the row of graves just before the steps to the royal tomb. Pull back the second grave on the right side of the path. Drop in. A Redead guards the room. Play the Sun's Song to freeze it, beat it, and open the chest. A free Hylian Shield! No rupees needed!
  28. Pull back the grave at the far south edge of that same row, below the royal tomb. Drop in. No bad guys here. Open the big chest for Heart Piece #12!
  29. Before you leave the graveyard, find the soft dirt patch up the steps on the left, past a small tree. Drop a bug from a bottle on it. A Gold Skulltula pops out! Bugs hide under stones at the graveyard door.
  30. Plant a Magic Bean NOW in that same soft dirt by the graveyard wall. As a grown-up you ride the big plant over the wall to Heart Piece #13!
  31. Now go up the trail to Death Mountain. The guard at the gate says the path is blocked. Falling rocks! Show him Zelda's Letter. He opens the gate for you.
  32. The guard also warns you about the rocks. He says to buy a Hylian Shield at the Bazaar in Castle Town. He even puts in a good word so the price drops from 80 rupees to 60 rupees! You already got one free in the graveyard, so you can skip it. He also asks you to check out the new Happy Mask Shop. Borrow the Keaton Mask there (worth 10 rupees) and sell it to this guard for 15 rupees!
KID TIP! Kakariko is full of cuccos (chickens). Do NOT hit them! If you hit one too much, a whole flock will attack you. It is funny, but it hurts. Also: the Sun's Song is your best friend in scary places. Redeads, Poes, and night time — one song fixes them all!
YOU GET: Epona's Song, Empty Bottle #2, Empty Bottle #3 (Lon Lon Milk), Sun's Song, Hylian Shield (free, in the graveyard tomb), Heart Pieces #3, #7, #11, #12

• Heart Pieces #11 and #12: In the graveyard — Dampé's digging tour and the tomb chest.
